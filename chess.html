Practice board

Choose local two-player practice or a computer opponent. Computer levels now explain what changes: Easy plays randomly, Medium looks one move ahead, and Difficult searches several half-moves ahead. For online games, use the separate multiplayer room lobby.

Easy: unpredictable casual moves. Medium: chooses a strong-looking move with a one-move material check. Difficult: searches deeper, so it may take longer on phones.
