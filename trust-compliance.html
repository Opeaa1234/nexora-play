Regulatory readiness

We are identifying the applicable requirements before introducing any real-money gaming feature.

  • Confirm the correct licence category with the relevant regulator.
  • Verify company, capital and application requirements.
  • Confirm technical testing, age checks, KYC and AML obligations.
  • Obtain advice for each additional country before launch there.

Lagos State Lotteries and Gaming Authority

Player safety

Before any regulated gaming launch, applicable safeguards will be documented and implemented.

  • Age and location eligibility checks.
  • Clear game rules, fees and payout conditions.
  • Complaint and dispute-handling process.
  • Responsible-gaming information and controls required by law.

Privacy and security

Nexora Play uses player accounts and profile information. A complete privacy notice and data-handling procedures should be published before collecting data beyond what is necessary for the service.

  • Explain what information is collected and why.
  • Set retention, access and deletion procedures.
  • Protect account access and restrict staff access.
  • Assess applicable Nigerian data-protection obligations.

Nigeria Data Protection Commission

Payments and real-money features

Real-money staking is not represented as available or authorized. Before any launch, Nexora Play must confirm the necessary gaming permissions, use payment providers that support the licensed activity, and publish clear rules covering deposits, stakes, cancellations, refunds, disputes and withdrawals.

Approval and verification

Nexora Play will only display a government approval badge, licence number or regulator logo when authorization has actually been granted and the details can be verified. This page is an information page, not a licence or endorsement.
