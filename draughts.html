How to play

  • Red moves first; pieces move diagonally forward.
  • Jump over an opponent to capture it. If a capture is available, you must take it.
  • Continue jumping with the same piece when another capture is available.
  • Reach the far edge to become a king. Kings move diagonally both ways.
