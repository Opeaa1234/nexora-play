# Nexora Play — Regulatory Preparation Checklist

**Purpose:** Prepare for a licensing discussion with the Lagos State Lotteries and Gaming Authority (LSLGA). This checklist is for planning only and does not establish that Nexora Play is licensed or that a particular licence category applies.

**Current planned model:** Online chess and draughts, with a proposed player-versus-player cash-staking feature. Initial target market: Nigeria.

## 1. Confirm the correct category before applying

- [ ] Send LSLGA a written description of the exact player-versus-player cash-staking model.
- [ ] Ask whether chess and draughts stakes are regulated as “Other Games,” interactive games, remote gaming, or another category.
- [ ] Ask whether separate approvals apply to the game operator, platform provider, payment arrangements, tournaments, or prize pools.
- [ ] Ask for the current application checklist, application fee, annual licence fee, tax/levy schedule, required capital and expected process.
- [ ] Ask whether the published “Requirements for Other Games Licence” PDF is current and applies to this product.

Official starting points:
- LSLGA: https://lslga.org/
- Gaming categories: https://lslga.org/gaming-categories/
- LSLGA contact: info@lslga.org

## 2. Company and ownership documents

Confirm the current requirements with LSLGA and a Nigerian lawyer/accountant.

- [ ] CAC incorporation documents and company registration details.
- [ ] Current share capital and ownership requirements.
- [ ] Directors’ and beneficial owners’ details and identification documents, as required.
- [ ] Registered office and contact details.
- [ ] Source-of-funds and financial-capacity documents, if required.
- [ ] Business plan and management/operational structure.
- [ ] Any required local-shareholding, guarantee or surety arrangements, confirmed against the current rules.

Do not assume historical figures in a downloaded PDF are still current. Get written confirmation before committing funds.

## 3. Product and player-protection documentation

- [ ] Product description and flow diagrams for account creation, matchmaking, staking, game result, settlement, refunds and disputes.
- [ ] Complete game rules and match-cancellation procedures.
- [ ] Proposed fee, stake and payout model.
- [ ] Age-verification and player eligibility plan.
- [ ] Know Your Customer (KYC) and anti-money-laundering (AML) procedures, as applicable.
- [ ] Responsible-gaming policy, limits and self-exclusion process, as required.
- [ ] Complaints, dispute resolution and customer support procedures.
- [ ] Fraud prevention, collusion detection and match-integrity procedures.

## 4. Technical and security requirements

The LSLGA “Other Games” requirements document lists technical-partner information, ISO 27001:2013 certification for technical partners, platform architecture, hosting details, data-centre documentation, backup/business-continuity and data-protection/recovery policies, accounting software, hardware/software specifications, ISO 9001:2015 for quality assurance and GLI 19 platform certification.

For each item, ask LSLGA to confirm whether it applies to Nexora Play and which current certification standard/version they accept.

- [ ] Technical architecture diagram, including frontend, backend, database and realtime match service.
- [ ] Hosting provider and data-centre information.
- [ ] Technical partner/developer details and service responsibilities.
- [ ] Backup, disaster recovery and business-continuity policies.
- [ ] Data-protection and recovery procedures.
- [ ] Security controls, access management, monitoring and incident response.
- [ ] Technical testing and independent certification requirements.
- [ ] Audit logs for stakes, game results, balances and payouts.
- [ ] Written confirmation of the required GLI standard and approved testing laboratory.

**Current technical note:** Nexora Play is hosted as a GitHub Pages website and uses Supabase for account/database services. The current room feature is not a complete certified real-money gaming platform. Do not enable cash stakes or real-money deposits/withdrawals while approvals and technical requirements are unresolved.

## 5. Financial, tax and payment requirements

- [ ] Ask LSLGA for current licence fees, renewals, taxes, levies, guarantees and capital requirements for the confirmed category.
- [ ] Prepare financial projections and source-of-funds documentation if requested.
- [ ] Confirm how player funds must be held and reconciled.
- [ ] Obtain written confirmation from a payment provider that it supports the licensed activity.
- [ ] Document refunds, chargebacks, withdrawals, failed transactions and dispute handling.
- [ ] Get advice on Nigerian tax, accounting and reporting obligations.

## 6. Privacy and website policies

- [ ] Publish a truthful privacy notice and contact route.
- [ ] Document data minimisation, retention, access, deletion and breach response.
- [ ] Review applicable Nigeria Data Protection Act/NDPC obligations and any registration or audit requirements.
- [ ] Publish terms of service, game rules, complaints policy and responsible-gaming information before any regulated launch.
- [ ] Display a licence number or government badge only after approval is actually granted and verifiable.

NDPC: https://ndpc.gov.ng/

## 7. Launch controls

- [ ] Keep real-money staking, deposits and withdrawals disabled until the applicable authorisation and safeguards are confirmed.
- [ ] Free-play development may continue, subject to applicable laws and appropriate privacy/security practices.
- [ ] Obtain legal/regulatory advice before opening in each additional Nigerian state or another country.
- [ ] Keep copies of regulator correspondence, applications, approvals and compliance evidence.

## First email to send

Subject: Confirmation of licence category and current requirements for player-versus-player cash-staked chess and draughts

Dear Lagos State Lotteries and Gaming Authority,

I am developing Nexora Play, an online gaming platform planned to launch in Nigeria. The proposed product includes chess and draughts where players may stake money against each other based on match outcomes.

Before enabling real-money play, deposits or withdrawals, please confirm which licence or permit category applies to this specific model. In particular, please advise whether the “Other Games” requirements document applies, whether a separate category applies to player-versus-player cash staking, and which technical certification standards, company-capital requirements, fees, tax/levy obligations and player-protection controls are currently required.

Please also confirm that the published downloadable requirements are current and provide the official application checklist for the correct category.

Kind regards,
Nexora Play

---
This is a working checklist, not legal advice or a completed licence application. Requirements must be confirmed with LSLGA and qualified Nigerian advisers before Nexora Play operates real-money gaming.
